    Score formula and weights
    score = signal (RSRP, SINR) + available capacity + expected dwell + service suitability + energy (battery) + network preference − latency − congestion (load) − handover frequency · weights × service multipliers, normalised to 0–100

    Handover rules editable

    Trigger only if the target meets the minimum service requirements, beats the serving score by the margin, holds that for the time-to-trigger, and has enough predicted dwell; the previous node is blocked for the ping-pong window. If the serving node is predicted to cross its threshold within 5 s, the margin is relaxed (predictive handover).
